How We Partner

Performance Partnership

Closed Deals on 50/50 Split

We run full-cycle sales for commercial cleaners: outbound prospecting, virtual walkthroughs, qualification, and DocuSign close. All selling is done for you—building trust and answering basic questions. You pay us 50% of month 1 and 50% of month 2—only when you collect from the client. After month 2, you keep 100% of all future payments.

Why Partner With Us

Paid on Collections, Not Signatures

We only get paid when you collect from the client. If they don't pay month 2, we don't get that second 50%. Fully aligned incentives.

Virtual Walkthroughs, Not Cold Meetings

Recorded Whereby sessions qualify prospects before you ever send a quote. Higher close rates, less wasted time on bad fits.

You Keep 100% After Month 2

We split months 1–2 at 50/50. Months 3–12? All yours. No recurring commission, no long-term obligations.

How Our Partnership Works

  • ✔ Partner Vetting: We work with established commercial cleaners with $1M+ insurance, active staff, and proven close rates.
  • ✔ Targeted Outbound & Selling: We handle all prospecting, cold outreach, virtual qualification, and answer basic facility questions so all selling is done for you.
  • ✔ Optional On-Site Handshake: We only ask you or your team to go on-site if the client wants to meet someone to verify you're legit and to hand over keys.
  • ✔ Close & Split: We send DocuSign, track to signature, and split months 1–2 at 50/50—paid only when you collect from the client. After month 2, you keep 100%.
