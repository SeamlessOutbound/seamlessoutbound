Frequently Asked Questions

Does Scout book appointments automatically?

No. You or your caller make the calls and have the conversations. Walkthrough Scout organizes the prospecting, dialing, notes, and callback alerts around that work so no lead falls through the cracks.

Do I need to buy a separate lead list?

No separate lead-list subscription is required. You search for local businesses on Google Maps, Yelp, or BBB, and Walkthrough Scout captures the available details into your side-panel workspace.

Who should actually make the calls?

Start by making the dials yourself for 30 minutes a day to establish the habit. Once you understand the simple calling routine, you can easily hand the exact Scout workspace off to a part-time cleaner on commission or a virtual caller off Upwork.
