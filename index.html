Why I built Walkthrough Scout

I Needed a Better Calling Workflow. So I Built One.

I'm Floyd—a salesperson and JavaScript developer. I've done the work of finding businesses, picking up the phone, and following up.

I used Google Maps, Yelp, and BBB to find businesses to contact. But the prospecting process felt like a patchwork: find the information in one place, use another tool to call, record the conversation somewhere else, then keep track of the callback.

I didn't want another complicated system to manage. I wanted a practical tool that kept those steps together and helped me stay focused on the next conversation.

That's why I built Walkthrough Scout. It's designed around the day-to-day prospecting work commercial cleaners actually need to do—not around adding more software to their workload.

A Few Questions Before You Start

Does Scout book appointments automatically?

No. You or your caller make the calls and have the conversations. Scout helps organize the prospecting, calling, notes, and follow-up around that work.

Do I need to buy a separate lead list?

No separate lead-list subscription is required for the directory prospecting workflow shown in the demonstration. You identify businesses to contact and use Scout to capture available details and manage your outreach.

Are the businesses already looking for cleaning services?

Not necessarily. A business listing is a prospect, not a confirmed cleaning enquiry. Your outreach helps you find out who handles cleaning, whether there is a need, and when to follow up.
