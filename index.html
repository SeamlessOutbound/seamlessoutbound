The Habit-Building Roadmap

Don't Start with 4 Hours on the Phone. Start with 30 Minutes.

Most cleaning owners stall out on outbound calls because they try to force a massive marathon on day one and burn out. Scout helps you stack small, repeatable daily blocks instead.

Phase 1: Week 1

30 Mins / Day

Set a single 30-minute sprint block. Pull 15 local prospects off Google Maps and make your calls before your daily jobs or walkthroughs pick up.

Phase 2: Weeks 2–3

Two 30-Min Blocks

Once your first block feels natural, add a second short block later in the day to follow up on callbacks and track walkthroughs.

Phase 3: Scale or Delegate

Up to ~2 Hours / Day

Keep your commercial pipeline moving in short daily sessions—and when you're ready, hand the exact Scout workspace to an internal caller or assistant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Scout book appointments automatically?

No. You or your caller make the calls and have the conversations. Walkthrough Scout organizes your local prospects, notes, sprint timers, and callback reminders so follow-ups stay organized.

Do I need to buy a separate lead list?

No separate lead-list subscription is required. Search local businesses on Google Maps, Yelp, or BBB and capture available contact details straight into your side-panel workspace.

Who should make the calls?

Start with a 30-minute daily block yourself to build the habit and hear how real conversations go. Once the routine feels natural, you can easily hand the workspace to someone on your team or a dedicated caller.
